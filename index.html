Teslabot site

A free, private home for your Tesla bot's public key.

Why this exists

Tesla's official Fleet API lets you read your own Powerwall, solar and car data. The catch is that each app has to publish a public key on a website it controls.

Most people don't have a website, and setting one up is a faff. This template gives you one for free on GitHub Pages in a couple of minutes.

Why it's good

  • 💷Free. No domain to buy and no hosting bill.
  • ✅Official. It uses Tesla's own API, not an unofficial workaround.
  • 🔒Private. No accounts, tracking, cookies or scripts from other sites. Your private key and your data never live here.
  • 🧩Simple. Just a public key plus a sign-in page that shows you a code to copy.
  • 👀Open. All the code is in plain view on GitHub.

How it works

  1. Your bot makes a key pair and publishes only the public half here.
  2. You register this site with Tesla.
  3. You sign in with Tesla, this site shows you a one-time code on its sign-in page, and you give it to your bot.
